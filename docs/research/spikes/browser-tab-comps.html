2 · A tab that came back without loading

After a relaunch, an internet tab keeps its address but fetches nothing until you ask (the issue's "restore without silent egress"). Local tabs load at once and never show this. Shown in the A1 row.

3 · When an agent is driving the tab

A chat's agent opened or is reading this page. Session Ink is a session's mark and nothing else (DESIGN.md, the Two Hands Rule), so each option uses it only as a mark, never as a button or a fill. Each shows the moment of reading; C2 also shows what stays after.

C1A notice strip

The Question Notice's shape: a one-row strip under the address, a Session Ink glyph, who is doing what, and a way to the chat. Most explicit; costs a row of page height while it shows.

4 · How you open one

Links in chats and documents open a tab on their own. For a blank one: ⌘T in the desktop app (free in both the menu and the SPA today) and a Go to command in both modes. The question was whether the desk also shows a button; the maintainer asked for one at the strip's right end, as cmux has (E3).

E1⌘T and Go to only

No new chrome on any pane. "New browser tab" sits in Go to's Commands with its chord; ⌘T opens it directly. The new tab arrives with the address field focused. Kept as the keyboard path beside E3b.

Drawn in A1. These follow from the issue and the security rules.
