1 · Font pairs. Same document, same proportions (glosa's shipped 18px / 1.62 / 68ch), only the faces change.

Each card is 68ch of its own body face, so widths differ a little: that is what a real measure does. Serifs all set at 18px, so x-height differences show as size differences; the sans body option sets at 16px as glosa's own Sans face does. Faces come from Google Fonts for this comparison only; the product vendors OFL woff2 files.

2 · Proportions. Same pair (Source Serif 4 + Source Sans 3), only sizes, weights and measure change.

Watch the h4 under the body text, the step between h2 and h3, and how much the title dominates a screen. The h1 is shown at its widest-pane size; glosa scales it down with the pane.

4 · Chat. The same exchange under candidate treatments; the manuscript beside it is 18px serif.

What ships: replies unboxed in Source Serif 4 at 15px / 1.7 on a 46rem column, the human turn in a right-aligned sans bubble, Markdown headings inside replies in sans. Variants change size, faces, boxing and heading treatment only. The Claude-like card approximates claude.ai's serif-reply register with Source Serif 4; it is not Anthropic's face.

5 · Registers (shipped in #407 as styles). A product spec in Editorial (Serif), in Spec (Sans) as decided, and the alternatives that were not chosen.

A register is the face plus its scale: body size, leading, heading sizes and weights, table and code treatment. Decided (2026-09-27, #407), named in the product as a style: Editorial (Serif) is Source Serif 4 18 / 1.62 on 68ch, headings 40 / 26 / 20 at 650 / 620 / 620, and h4 to h6 at the body's 18px in 650. Spec (Sans) is Source Sans 3 16 / 1.5 on 64ch, h1 32, h2 24, h3 20, h4 17, h5 and h6 16, all 650, tables at the body's 16px with a 650 head, inline code 0.9em, code blocks 14 / 1.5, and tables and code free to widen to about 96ch. Mono keeps the mono face as it was. The 18px Spec, IBM Plex Sans and Inter cards are the alternatives that were not chosen. Section 1 shows the essay in Editorial.

6 · A Spec page with the chat beside it. Does the chat keep its own serif style, or follow the page into sans?

Top pair (decided): the chat owns one Conversation style (Source Serif 4 16 / 1.62) beside every register, like margin notes. Lower pair: the chat follows the adjacent document's register, so beside a Spec page its replies turn Source Sans 3 16 / 1.5. The person's turn stays a sans bubble in both, so only the reply face differs.

3 · Palettes and themes, ported onto glosa's semantic tokens. Toggle Light / Dark above.

Every card keeps glosa's rule: the human's mark is one colour (the hand), an unsent mark is pencil, a session's mark is a second colour (session ink). For editor palettes the hand takes the palette's orange and the session its blue, the closest honest mapping. The caption reports WCAG contrast of ink, muted, hand and session on the ground, and the hue gap between hand and session (below about 60 degrees the two hands start to read as one).

7 · High contrast light and dark, as #409 ships them, beside glosa's own. Toggle Light / Dark above.

The values are the theme files in packages/spa/src/themes/, copied here. Each role is measured on the grounds it is actually drawn on (validate.js's table, copied below): a greyed ratio is a ground the role never sits on, shown for comparison only. Floors are WCAG 2 ratios: ink 7:1; muted 4.5:1 (7:1 in High contrast); the hand, session ink, danger, warn and ok 4.5:1 as text and 3:1 as a line (4.5:1 as a line in High contrast); pencil 4.5:1; the strong border 3:1. High contrast keeps glosa's paper and surfaces, so a desktop window opened under Increase Contrast starts on the right paper, and deepens (light) or lifts (dark) the ink, the marks and the edges within their own hues. The colour-vision rows simulate protanopia, deuteranopia and tritanopia with the Machado, Oliveira and Fernandes (2009) matrices at full severity, applied in linear sRGB; ΔE is the OKLab distance between two colours as a person with that vision would see them (about 0.02 is just noticeable). Section 3's High contrast card is the study's earlier proposal, not these values.
